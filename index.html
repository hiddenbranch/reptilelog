          What happens next

          1. A parent repliesTo confirm your dates or ask a question.
          2. Free meet-and-greet at your home and a parent meet your animals and walk through your routine. We write it up as a care sheet for each animal, with your vet and who to call.
          3. Visits while you're awayEvery visit is logged: what was offered, what was eaten, temperatures, anything unusual. You get a photo and a note each time. If something looks wrong, a parent calls you right away.
          4. You come home to a care reportThe whole log in one place, so you can see exactly what happened while you were gone.

          What we say no to

            Monitors and other big, strong animals are a maybe: we'll want to meet yours first.

            Need to rehome a reptile?

            If you have a reptile you can't keep any more, please don't release it. Pets rarely survive outside, and some cause real trouble for local wildlife.

            Tell us about the animal and we'll talk it through. We're still setting this up, so it always starts with a conversation before any animal changes hands. We can't take every animal, and some species need permits we don't hold.
